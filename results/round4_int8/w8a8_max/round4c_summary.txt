Conclusion
----------
Max-range W8A8 post-training quantisation retained nearly all
FP32 reconstruction quality and remained superior to bicubic
interpolation on all 20 held-out validation images.

This remains a fake-quantisation simulation. The convolution
operations themselves are still performed using floating-point
PyTorch arithmetic after dequantisation.

The next experiment therefore evaluates integer convolution
accumulators and quantised biases.
