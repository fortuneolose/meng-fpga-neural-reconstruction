Conclusion
----------
Weight-only INT8 post-training quantisation preserved the FP32
model's reconstruction performance extremely well on the 20-image
held-out validation set.

The model retained its advantage over bicubic interpolation on all
20 validation images.

This experiment simulates INT8 weight representation by dequantising
the stored INT8 weights for floating-point PyTorch execution. It does
not yet represent bit-exact integer convolution or FPGA inference.
