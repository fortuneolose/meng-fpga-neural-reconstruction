Interpretation
--------------
The observed validation-set accumulator peaks are not sufficient
for hardware sizing.

The guaranteed bounds use the actual quantised INT8 weights,
maximum permitted activation values and quantised biases.

For the first FPGA/HLS implementation, INT32 accumulators will be
used for simplicity and verification margin.

The reduced widths 19/22/21 bits are candidates for later hardware
optimisation after bit-exact equivalence is established.
