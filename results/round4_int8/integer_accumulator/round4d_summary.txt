Conclusion
----------
Integer accumulation with quantised biases preserved the W8A8
quality result and remained above bicubic on all 20 held-out images.

Requantisation is still performed using floating-point scale
multiplication. The next stages are:

1. derive guaranteed accumulator bounds;
2. replace floating-point requantisation with integer
   multiplier-and-shift arithmetic;
3. quantise the remaining skip/add datapath.
